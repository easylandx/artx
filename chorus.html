Artist Statement

Fracture Choir Pop

Chorus Line of Traffic Lights stages seven intersection signals as a vaudeville troupe — red, yellow, and green not as instructions but as vocal registers. Where a real intersection enforces silence and sequence, this chorus fractures the rule: the lights sing out of turn, overlap, harmonize, and occasionally all shout at once.

The piece belongs to a movement Pryce calls Fracture Choir Pop — compositions built from civic infrastructure re-cast as performers, where the "fracture" is the gap between a signal's intended function and its improvised, theatrical misuse. Traffic control becomes stagecraft; the crosswalk becomes a wing.

Visitors are asked to conduct, not just watch. Every click is a cue. No two performances repeat the same line twice.

— N. Pryce, 2026
