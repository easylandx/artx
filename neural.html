Artist statement

A mind remembering itself
through broken light.

Neural Echoes imagines the interior of a synthetic mind as a drowned city — corridors of thought folding into fog, memories surfacing as fractured architecture and dissolving before they can be named.

Elara Voss builds each piece from a live simulation: thousands of nodes fire, connect, and decay in real time, their light trails standing in for synapses that never quite finish a sentence. Cyber Surrealism, the movement Voss helped define, treats the network itself as landscape — dreamlike, glitch-prone, and always slightly out of focus.

Nothing on this page repeats exactly. Every reflection below is generated fresh, an echo of an echo, the way a memory changes shape each time it's recalled.
