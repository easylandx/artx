About Binary Garden

Binary Garden is Nova Kline's flagship exploration of code as living tissue — a generative ecosystem where circuit-traces behave like vines, capacitor-nodes pulse like bioluminescent spores, and every visitor's touch becomes a seed. No two visits produce the same garden; the piece is never finished, only cultivated.

Kline began the project after years of sketching root systems next to motherboard schematics, struck by how identically they branch toward resource and light. Binary Garden is the result: a canvas where lush emerald growth climbs over gold circuitry, lit from within by a soft bioluminescent blue, all rooted in deep forest shadow.

The work sits at the center of Digital Organicism — a movement Kline co-founded, treating computation not as sterile logic but as a growable, breathing organism.

Digital Organicism — a short manifesto

  • Code is not built. It is grown.
  • Every circuit is a root looking for light.
  • Interfaces should photosynthesize, not just render.
  • Glow is data given a pulse.
  • The viewer is a gardener, not an audience.

Garden News

MARCH 2026

Binary Garden opens at the Meridian Bio-Digital Biennial

Nova Kline unveils the full interactive build, letting biennial visitors plant the first ten thousand circuit-shoots live on opening night.

MAY 2026

Digital Organicism named "movement to watch" by New Media Quarterly

Critics highlight Binary Garden as the clearest articulation yet of code-as-organism aesthetics, praising its refusal to ever reach a final state.

JUNE 2026

Kline announces a traveling seed-archive of the garden

Snapshots of visitor-grown gardens will be preserved as a rotating archive, each one a fossil of a moment that can never regrow the same way twice.

JULY 2026

Collaboration teased with sound artist collective Mycelium//Signal

An expanded bioluminescent soundscape is in development, aiming to let the garden's growth patterns compose ambient tone in real time.
